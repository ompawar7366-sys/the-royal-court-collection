A Vision of Splendor

Founded in 1984, The Royal Court began as a modest boutique specializing in hand-cut sapphires. Today, we are a name synonymous with the finest diamonds and rare metalwork.

Our philosophy remains unchanged: to create objects of desire that transcend trends, becoming heirlooms that tell the unique stories of the families who possess them.
